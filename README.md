# Sample Superstore Sales Analysis | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyses the sales performance of a retail business using the Sample Superstore dataset. The objective is to identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, uncover areas of poor financial performance, and provide actionable business recommendations.

Using Microsoft Excel, I conducted an end-to-end analysis covering overall business performance, sales growth, product profitability, regional performance, customer segments, and the impact of discounting.

## Business Objectives

The analysis seeks to answer the following business questions:

1. What is the overall sales and profit performance of the business?
2. How have sales and profit changed over the years?
3. Which categories generate the most profit?
4. Which sub-categories generate losses?
5. Which regions perform best and worst in terms of profitability?
6. Which customer segments contribute most to sales and profit?
7. How do discounts affect profitability?
8. Which areas should management investigate first?

## Dataset Description

The project uses the Sample Superstore dataset, containing retail transaction records covering 2014–2017.

The dataset includes:
- Order and shipping dates
- Customer details
- Product categories and sub-categories
- Sales and profit
- Quantity sold
- Discounts
- Geographic information

## Analytical Process

**1. Data Preparation**
- Inspected the raw dataset.
- Prepared a separate clean-data worksheet.
- Organised the data for analysis.

**2. Exploratory Data Analysis**
- Evaluated overall sales, profit and order performance.
- Analysed annual and monthly trends.
- Examined product, regional and customer-segment performance.
- Investigated the relationship between discounting and profitability.

**3. Data Analysis**
- Created PivotTables to summarise business performance.
- Calculated profit margins and year-over-year growth.
- Identified loss-making products, sub-categories and states.

**4. Dashboard Development**
- Developed an Excel dashboard to communicate key performance indicators and business trends.

## Key Findings

1. **Overall Performance:** The business generated $2,297,201 in sales and $286,397 in profit from 5,009 distinct orders, representing an overall profit margin of approximately 12.5%.

2. **Business Growth:** Sales increased by 51.4% and profit by 88.6% between 2014 and 2017.

3. **Seasonality:** November, December and September recorded the strongest sales, while February was the weakest month.

4. **Category Performance:** Technology and Office Supplies generated 93.6% of total profit. Furniture contributed 32.3% of sales but only 6.4% of profit.

5. **Sub-category Profitability:** Tables, Bookcases and Supplies recorded overall losses. Machines generated a relatively low profit margin of 1.8%.

6. **Regional Performance:** The West region recorded the highest profit margin at 14.9%, while Central recorded the lowest at 7.9%.

7. **Customer Segments:** Profit margins across customer segments ranged from 11.5% to 14.0%.

8. **Discount Impact:** Products discounted by more than 20% collectively generated losses of $135,376, while undiscounted sales recorded a 29.5% profit margin.


## Business Recommendations

- **Review discount policies:** Introduce stricter controls on high discount levels and evaluate their impact on profit margins.
- **Improve product profitability:** Investigate losses in Tables, Bookcases and Supplies, reviewing pricing, procurement costs and discount strategies.
- **Optimise regional performance:** Investigate the factors contributing to Central's low profitability, particularly its relatively high average discount.
- **Review underperforming products:** Assess products with recurring losses or very low margins.
- **Leverage seasonal demand:** Align inventory planning and promotional activities with months of stronger sales.
